# Project Vision — Centre for Advanced Studies ERP

## Vision
Deliver a secure, bilingual, responsive, integrated college ERP that supports academic and administrative operations across the Centre for Advanced Studies, with a configurable AKTU-oriented academic model and a staged path to enterprise operation.

## Outcomes
1. Establish one authoritative, auditable source for institutional records.
2. Reduce duplicate data entry and manual reconciliation.
3. Give students, parents, faculty and administrators role-appropriate self-service.
4. Improve visibility into attendance, fees, examinations, HR, assets and institutional performance.
5. Provide controlled integration with payment, messaging, identity, accounting and authorized university systems.
6. Support reliable migration from the existing ERP/database.
7. Provide operational resilience, security governance and measurable service quality.

## Product boundaries
- Initial scope: one college, one campus.
- Responsive web portal; no separate native Android/iOS apps in initial scope. PWA may be considered later.
- Configurable program/course structures; do not invent actual course offerings.
- AKTU alignment is configurable and depends on verified rules and authorized interfaces.
- AI is assistive and advisory, never the final decision-maker for high-impact matters.

## Success measures
Establish baselines during discovery. Proposed measures include successful migration reconciliation, completion of critical user journeys, reduction in duplicate records, payment reconciliation accuracy, report generation time, system availability, support response/resolution, user adoption and accessibility conformance. Numeric SLOs must be agreed before production.
