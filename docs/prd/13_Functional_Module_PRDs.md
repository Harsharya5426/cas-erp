# Functional Module PRDs

This file provides the functional baseline for all selected modules. Detailed workflows, field-level schemas and institution-specific rules are refined during discovery.

## 1. Academic Management
Configurable institution → department → program → course → session/semester → batch/section → subject hierarchy. Support UG, PG, professional and future program types without assuming actual course catalogue. Semester enrollment, credits, subject/faculty mapping, promotions, backlogs and academic history.

## 2. AKTU-Oriented Academic Configuration
Configurable AKTU-aligned curriculum, registration, syllabus, credit mapping, examination/admit-card and regulatory reporting. Official university integrations only with documented API and permission. Rules must be versioned and updated by authorized administrators.

## 3. Admissions and Website
Public CMS, SEO, notices, online/offline application, application number, document upload/verification, merit/eligibility, seat allocation, approvals, admission fee, enrollment conversion, cancellation/refund and applicant communications.

## 4. Student Attendance
Lecture-wise subject/semester attendance; faculty marking; face/QR/RFID/manual/approved biometric options; configurable thresholds; correction and approval; shortage/defaulter reporting and alerts.

## 5. Employee Attendance and HR
Employee profiles, onboarding/exit, departments/designations, recruitment, documents, attendance, shifts, leave, workload, performance reviews, configurable payroll, payslips and notices.

## 6. Employee Leave
Configurable CL, SL, EL, medical, maternity, paternity, unpaid and special leave; balances; half/full days; applications; approvals; holiday calendar; attendance sync and reports.

## 7. Student Leave and Permission
Leave requests, supporting documents, approval chain, late entry, early departure, hostel outpass, parent approval where configured, notifications and attendance integration.

## 8. Fees and Finance
Program/semester fee plans, installments, scholarships/concessions, late fees, offline receipts, Cashfree payments, refunds, invoices/receipts, dues, defaulter reports, reconciliation, ledgers and audit.

## 9. Accounting
Double-entry ledger, journals, vouchers, receipts, expenses, cash/bank books, reconciliation, budgets, cost centres, vendor payments and finance reports. GST/TDS-ready configurable workflows only where applicable and verified by finance professionals.

## 10. Examinations and Results
Exam schedules, rooms/invigilators, internal assessment, practical/project/quiz marks, configurable grades/GPA/CGPA, admit-card eligibility, verification, publication, marksheets, backlogs, supplementary exams, revaluation, transcripts and audit-locked results.

## 11. Timetable
Manual timetable creation, faculty substitutions, rooms/labs, workload, student views, calendar integration, notifications and conflict detection across faculty, rooms, labs, batches and holidays.

## 12. Library
Catalogue, ISBN, author/publisher/category, issue/return/renewal, transaction history, barcode/QR, overdue fines, reservations, digital resources and analytics.

## 13. Hostel and Mess
Multiple hostels/buildings/floors/rooms/beds; applications and approvals; allotment/change/vacating; fees/deposits/fines; visitor records; attendance/outpass; mess menu/meal plans; complaints, maintenance, inventory and notices.

## 14. Student and Parent Portals
Student profile, attendance, fees, receipts, timetable, syllabus/resources, assignments, exam schedule/admit cards/results, leave/certificate requests, grievances and notices. Parent accounts require verified relationship and scoped access.

## 15. Documents and Certificates
Student/employee document repository, verification, categories, version history, permissions, certificate requests and generation (e.g. bonafide, character, transfer, migration), authorized digital signing and QR verification. AI extraction requires human verification.

## 16. Placement and Career
Employer registration, jobs/internships, eligibility, applications, drives, interviews, offers, placement records, resume builder, skill profiles, aptitude preparation, career guidance, training and analytics.

## 17. Alumni
Registration, searchable directory, profiles, networking, events, mentorship, referrals, donations/scholarships, career opportunities and analytics with privacy preferences.

## 18. Inventory and Assets
Procurement, vendors, purchase approvals, stock, asset allocation, lab inventory, barcode/QR, maintenance, depreciation, stock audits, alerts and analytics.

## 19. Grievance and Helpdesk
Confidential submissions, categories, restricted assignment, escalation, appeals, department helpdesk, SLA tracking, AI classification/response suggestions and analytics. Sensitive matters remain human-led and access-restricted.

## 20. Events and Campus Activities
Event planning, registrations, approvals, venue booking, budgets, attendance, certificates, clubs, societies, competitions, workshops, seminars, cultural and sports events.

## 21. Basic LMS
Course resources, PDF notes, study materials, assignments, submissions, evaluation/feedback, announcements and faculty tools. Advanced live classrooms, AI tutoring and integrated online exams are outside initial LMS scope.

## 22. Research and Innovation
Proposals, approvals, grants/funding, budgets, progress reports, publications, patents, lab management, collaborations, IP, ethics approvals, conferences and innovation activity.

## 23. Sports and Fitness
Registrations, teams, coaches, facilities, competitions, tournaments, results, achievements, equipment and physical education records.

## 24. Safety and Visitor Register
Emergency contacts, safety notices, incident records and basic visitor name/contact/purpose/entry/exit/history. Collect only necessary visitor data and define retention.

## 25. Reports and Business Continuity
Advanced role-scoped reporting, filters, custom reports, scheduled exports, dashboards, automated distribution, encrypted backups, PITR where supported, restore drills, failover and continuity plans.

## Excluded from initial scope
Dedicated transport-management module; native mobile apps; advanced LMS live classroom/AI tutor/online exam functions. Future scope requires separate approval.
